## Reserved slash commands

- `/settings`

## Notes

Skills are loaded from `~/.pi/agent/skills/`, `.pi/skills/`, `.agents/skills/`, and via `--skill`. Skills are invoked with `/skill:name`; `/settings` toggles skill commands.
