# Related skills

- `investigate-first` — gather evidence before scoping.
- `minimal-root-cause` — avoid speculative restructuring disguised as refactoring.
- `one-shot-patch` — use for narrow behavior-preserving edits that are not part of a larger refactor.
- `architecture-review` — produce the diagnosis that motivates a refactor.
- `codehome` — corrective re-placement of misplaced code, often a step inside refactoring.
- `evidence` — record the test runs that prove no behavior changed.
- `git-workflow-and-versioning` — atomic, scoped commits and safe pushes per step.
